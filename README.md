## What Is Face AI Tracker?

Face AI Tracker is a real-time emotion detection wellness web application deployed at [facewellnessai.com](https://facewellnessai.com).

It uses your device camera to detect your facial expressions in real time, identify your current emotional state, and provide wellness coaching responses tailored to how you are actually feeling at that moment — not how you say you feel.

The AI doesn't ask you how you are. It reads your face and responds to what it sees.

---

## What It Does

The application activates your camera, analyzes your facial expression in real time, detects your emotional state — happy, sad, stressed, tired, neutral, and more — and responds with a wellness coaching message designed for that specific emotion.

It tracks blink rate as a fatigue indicator. It works on desktop browsers and is being optimized for mobile iOS and Android with improved response and stability.

---

## Why It Exists

Most wellness apps ask you to self-report your mood. Self-reporting is inaccurate — people lie to themselves, minimize how they feel, or simply don't know. Face AI Tracker removes that barrier by reading emotion directly from the face, making the coaching response more honest and more relevant.
